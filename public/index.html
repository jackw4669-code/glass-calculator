Glass

No sizes yet? Tick needs measuring first under Handover and save — the job goes in the diary to be measured, and you add the sizes afterwards.

Frames

The price is whatever you are charging, typed in by hand. Tick Needs to order? on a frame coming from a supplier: once the customer confirms, it goes to To be ordered, then On order.

Maintenance

Each thing being done or fitted, how many, and the price each before VAT. Tick Needs to order? on anything that has to come in: once the customer confirms, it goes to To be ordered, then On order.

Photos

Photos of the opening, the damage or the old frame. Anything ticked Send is offered to the supplier with the price request or the order. They are shrunk before they are saved, so they do not slow the job book down.

Extras and labour

Nothing will be added for VAT and the quote will not mention it. Only use this where the work genuinely is not standard-rated.

Payments

Deposits, card payments, transfers. Whatever is left shows as the balance on the job card and in the money owed list.

Handover

The whole job turns red on the list and in the diary, and the note is shown large. Use it for the ones that will go wrong if somebody misses the note.
